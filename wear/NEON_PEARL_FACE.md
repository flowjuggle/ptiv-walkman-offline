# PTIV Neon Pearl Triangle — isolated watch face

Package: `live.ptiv.faces.neonpearl` (new package, independent of original Pearl, Night and Pulse watch faces).

The tap target launches `live.ptiv.watch.neonpreview/live.ptiv.watch.WatchActivity`, the already-installed P.T. Architech Neon Test player. The original Walkman and its shortcut remain in place, and no audio is embedded or modified.

Safety: verify the APK SHA-256 and applicationId before install. Do not uninstall existing watch faces or the original Walkman. The existing three faces still open the original Walkman. This extra face opens the Neon player only. Do not publish the prototype as a production artifact until signing and full tests are complete.
